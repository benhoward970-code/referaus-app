─────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT IS IT?
─────────────────────────────────────────

ReferAus is a marketplace for professional referrals. It lets people search for, compare, read reviews on, and directly contact service providers in their area — no middlemen, no cold calls, no guesswork.

The platform launched in the NDIS (National Disability Insurance Scheme) space, which is the perfect proving ground:

  • 600,000+ Australians rely on NDIS funding
  • $42 billion annual market
  • Participants genuinely struggle to find and compare providers
  • No dominant marketplace exists in this space

The web app is already live and production-ready at referaus.com.

─────────────────────────────────────────
HOW IT MAKES MONEY
─────────────────────────────────────────

Free for participants. Providers pay a monthly subscription:

  Free      — $0/mo      Basic listing, appear in search
  Starter   — ~$29/mo    Enhanced profile, priority listing
  Pro       — ~$79/mo    Analytics, featured placement
  Premium   — ~$149/mo   Top placement, verified badge, API access

Recurring monthly revenue that scales with the provider base.
