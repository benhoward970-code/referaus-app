The Concept

What is ReferAus?

ReferAus is an Australian marketplace for professional referrals. At its core, it connects people who need a service with verified, reviewed providers — cutting out the guesswork, the cold calls, and the middlemen.

The platform launched in the NDIS (National Disability Insurance Scheme) space, which is a proven beachhead: the NDIS supports over 600,000 Australians and represents a $42 billion annual market. Participants constantly struggle to find, compare, and connect with suitable providers. ReferAus solves this directly.

But NDIS is just the start. The same problem — "I need to find a trusted professional in my area, read real reviews, and contact them directly" — exists across nearly every service industry in Australia.

💡 Think of it like a cross between HiPages, Google My Business, and a referral network — but purpose-built for industries where trust and credentials matter most.

Technology

Tech Stack

The platform is built on a modern, scalable stack that any experienced developer can jump straight into:

Next.js 16 TypeScript Supabase PostgreSQL Stripe Resend Tailwind CSS v4 Framer Motion Vercel GitHub

Everything is deployed on Vercel with CI/CD via GitHub. The database is Supabase (PostgreSQL) with row-level security, real-time capabilities, and built-in auth. It's designed to scale horizontally from day one.

Revenue Model

How It Makes Money

ReferAus is free for participants (people seeking services). Revenue comes from provider subscriptions — businesses pay a monthly fee to access premium visibility, enquiry management, and analytics.

Plan Monthly Key Features Target
Free $0 Basic listing, appear in search Acquisition
Starter ~$29 Enhanced profile, priority listing, enquiries SMBs
Pro ~$79 Analytics, featured placement, reviews management Growth
Premium ~$149 Top placement, verified badge, advanced analytics, API Enterprise

This model scales cleanly: as the provider base grows, recurring monthly revenue grows proportionally. It also creates low churn — providers who are listed and getting enquiries have strong incentive to stay.

The Big Picture

The Vision: Beyond NDIS

The NDIS market is the proving ground. The real opportunity is Australia-wide, across every service category where trust and credentials matter. The playbook is identical across all of them — list providers, verify credentials, collect real reviews, connect people.

  • 1
    Now · Phase 1
    NDIS Directory — Newcastle & Hunter Region

    Build the brand, prove the model, onboard the first 100 providers. Establish reviews and enquiry flow. Achieve product-market fit in a well-defined, underserved niche.

  • 2
    6–12 months · Phase 2
    National NDIS Expansion

    Roll out to all Australian states and territories. Target the 600,000+ NDIS participants and tens of thousands of registered providers nationally. Establish ReferAus as the go-to NDIS directory in Australia.

  • 3
    12–24 months · Phase 3
    Expand to Adjacent Health & Care Sectors

    Use the same platform to serve aged care, mental health practitioners, allied health, GP clinics, and other Medicare/regulated service categories. These sectors share the same problem: finding trusted, reviewed professionals is hard.

  • 4
    2+ years · Phase 4
    Australia's Referral Platform — Every Sector

    Expand beyond healthcare into legal, financial advice, education, trades, and professional services. Any service where trust, credentials, and reviews matter. A single trusted platform Australians use to find vetted professionals for anything.

📍 The name "ReferAus" was chosen deliberately — it's not "ReferNDIS" or "ReferHealth". It's built to be Australia's referral platform, full stop.

Market Size

The Opportunity

Each phase targets a distinct, large market. These are not speculative — they're existing, proven service categories with real spending:

NDIS · $42B/yr Aged Care · $24B/yr Allied Health · $19B/yr Mental Health Legal Services Financial Advice Education & Tutoring Trades & Home Services Childcare Veterinary

Existing competitors like HiPages and ServiceSeeking focus on trades and price-driven matching. ReferAus is positioned differently — trust, credentials, and relationship-based referrals, not just lowest-quote race-to-the-bottom. The closest comparison internationally is Zocdoc or Care.com, but there's no dominant player in this space in Australia.

The Ask

What I'm Looking For in a Dev Team

The web platform is live and functional. The next stage is building out the native mobile apps and scaling the backend to handle national growth. Here's what I need from a development partner:

📱 Native Mobile Apps

iOS and Android apps for both participants and providers. The web is a PWA but a proper native app is essential for adoption, push notifications, and app store presence.

🔔 Notifications & Messaging

Real-time in-app messaging between participants and providers. Push notifications for enquiry responses, new reviews, and account updates.

📊 Provider Dashboard

Analytics, enquiry management, review responses, and profile management — all in a clean native mobile experience for providers on the go.

🗺️ Location & Maps

Map-based provider search, location-aware results, and suburb/radius filtering. Critical for a national rollout where location matters.

🔐 Enhanced Auth

Biometric login, social sign-on (Google/Apple), and credential verification flows for registered providers (ABN, NDIS registration number).

⚙️ API Scalability

Optimise the existing Next.js API layer for national load. Caching, rate limiting, edge functions, and robust error handling at scale.
