Brand Overview

ReferAus is an Australian marketplace for professional referrals, starting with NDIS (National Disability Insurance Scheme) providers in the Hunter Region / Newcastle, NSW. It connects participants with trusted, verified service providers.

Personality: Trustworthy, approachable, modern, Australian. Not corporate or cold. Warm but professional.

Live URL: https://referaus.com — Next.js 16 app on Vercel.

💡 When designing for ReferAus: use orange as the primary action colour, blue as secondary/trust colour, and keep white-space generous. The audience includes people with disabilities — accessibility (contrast, focus states, large touch targets) is non-negotiable.

Design Priorities for Claude Design

When iterating on ReferAus designs, keep these in mind:

  1. Accessibility first — WCAG AA minimum contrast ratios. Large touch targets (44px+). Clear focus indicators.
  2. Mobile-first — Most NDIS participants browse on phones. Cards stack to single column below 640px.
  3. Trust signals — Verified badges, star ratings, review counts, and ABN verification are all critical for the NDIS audience.
  4. Clear CTAs — Every page needs one clear primary action in orange. Never compete with multiple CTAs of the same weight.
  5. Speed perception — Use skeleton loaders (not spinners) for async content. Cards should fill in progressively.
  6. Expansion mindset — Design components to work beyond NDIS (for aged care, mental health, etc.). Avoid hardcoding "NDIS" into UI chrome.
