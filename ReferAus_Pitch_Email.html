The Concept

What is ReferAus?

ReferAus is an Australian marketplace for professional referrals. It connects people who need a service with verified, reviewed providers — cutting out the guesswork, cold calls, and middlemen.

The platform launched in the NDIS space — a $42 billion annual market serving 600,000+ Australians who struggle to find and compare providers. But NDIS is just the start.

💡 Think of it like a cross between HiPages, Google My Business, and a referral network — purpose-built for industries where trust and credentials matter most.
