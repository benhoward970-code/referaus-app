## #10 — EDUCATION: REGISTERED VS UNREGISTERED (Instagram / Facebook)
*Value-add content — saves + shares*

> NDIS Registered vs Unregistered providers — what's the difference? 🤔
>
> ✅ REGISTERED providers:
> • Audited and approved by the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission
> • Required if you're plan-managed or agency-managed
> • Often have more capacity and formal processes
>
> ✅ UNREGISTERED providers:
> • Can still deliver great support
> • Only available if you're self-managed
> • Often more flexible and specialised
>
> On ReferAus, you can filter by registered providers specifically — so you're never left guessing. 🔍
>
> #NDIS #NDISEducation #NDISParticipant #NDISRegistered #HunterRegion #DisabilitySupport #ReferAus
